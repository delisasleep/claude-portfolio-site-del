Harjinder's Portfolio

A redesign of my sister's Wix site, built in close collaboration with her.

Harjinder's Portfolio moves her site off Wix and into a traditional two-track layout: a Portfolio section (magazine layouts, brand guides, packaging design, and other studio work) and a Digital Art section featuring her personal digital art ventures, built around texture, color palettes, blend modes, and object transformation experiments.

Bold colors carry the site throughout, chosen to match her visual style and give the brand more personality. Working this closely with a client on their own taste was a different kind of challenge than the solo projects, and it's worth keeping live and linked here alongside the newer work rather than replaced by it.
